THE STANDARD

Evidence should survive the conclusion.

A yellow3 finding should still make sense when somebody asks where did that come from? That means the source, date, uncertainty and later outcome remain visible.

01

Dated evidence

Sources are attached to the finding, not hidden behind the conclusion.

02

Measurable thesis

Where possible, the question is framed so reality can later prove it right or wrong.

03

Public grading

Calls are preserved and graded. The record does not reset when the story changes.

04

Visible uncertainty

Not established is different from disproven. The research should say which one it means.
