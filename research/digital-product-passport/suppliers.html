Evidence, not inference

Blank space is part of the map.

We do not infer a headquarters from a company name, domain ending, legal-form suffix, or regional office. If a location is not publicly sourced, the organisation stays off the map until it can be proven.

01Every location has a source.
02Every source has a date.

Corrections

Tell us what we got wrong.

Every finding here is a dated statement about published evidence, and every one of them can be wrong: a source we did not reach, a page that changed, a judgement we made badly. Corrections are free, they are never a commercial conversation, and you do not need to claim a profile to send one.

Send the supplier, the check or field, and a public URL with the sentence on that page that supports it. That is the same standard we hold ourselves to, and it is the only thing that lets us act on a correction the same day. Send a correction →
