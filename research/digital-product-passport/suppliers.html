How we verify

Every value in this register is backed by a page we opened, or it is left blank. A blank is not an oversight, it is a finding: it means the fact is not published anywhere we could reach on the date shown. Nothing here is inferred from a company name, a domain ending or a legal-form suffix.

VerifiedA legal document or register entry names the organisation - an imprint, legal notice, terms carrying a registered office, or a national business register.
ClaimedThe company states it about itself, and we link where. Recorded accurately, not endorsed.
Not foundWe looked on the date shown and the fact was not published. Dated, so you can see how fresh the check is.

Capability assessments run against ten independent checks, each with its own evidence and its own date. There is no composite score and no ranking. A supplier that has not been assessed says so; it is never shown as failing a check nobody ran.

Corrections

If something here is wrong, send the correction with a source and we will check it, fix it and log the change. Corrections are welcome from anyone, and we do not need you to be the company to send one.

Claiming a profile

If you work at one of these organisations you can claim your profile and supply your own logo, description and answers. Company-supplied content is shown in its own layer, marked as yours, and never overwrites what we verified independently.
