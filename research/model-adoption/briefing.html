Executive summary

Five conclusions, three minutes. What changed across model routing, economics and the European picture in August 2026.

  1. Asia holds the lead at 51.4% of routed tokens (-8.6 pp over four weeks) - the concentration of demand by region of origin is not easing.
  2. Anthropic Claude Opus 5 was the month's biggest climber, up 37 places to #13.
  3. 10 tracked model(s) cut price this month, extending the cost gap between low-cost challengers and premium frontier models.
  4. 12 new model(s) entered the ranking, led by DeepSeek V4 Flash (31 Jul 2026).
  5. Europe remains marginal at 0.22% of routed traffic; the regulatory picture is tracked in Europe Watch below.
