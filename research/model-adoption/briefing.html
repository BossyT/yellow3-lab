Executive summary

Five conclusions, three minutes. What changed across model routing, economics and the European picture in August 2026.

  1. Asia holds the lead at 59.9% of routed tokens (-3.9 pp over four weeks) - the concentration of demand by region of origin is not easing.
  2. Poolside Laguna S 2.1 (free) was the month's biggest climber, up 47 places to #10.
  3. 12 tracked model(s) cut price this month, extending the cost gap between low-cost challengers and premium frontier models.
  4. 9 new model(s) entered the ranking, led by DeepSeek V4 Flash.
  5. Europe remains marginal at 0.05% of routed traffic; the regulatory picture is tracked in Europe Watch below.
